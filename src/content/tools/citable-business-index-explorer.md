## What It Does

The **CITAble Business Index Explorer** turns the four-quadrant assessment into an interactive reading. It shows how a venture scores on each condition of future-proofing and how the sub-instruments combine into the integrating CBI measure.

## What It Reads

- **Cognitive Vitality Index**: the organisation's cognition.
- **Agentic Gradient**: the depth and governance of AI adoption.
- **Societal Resilience Index**: the venture's contribution to a stable society.
- **Active Futuring and Horizon Coherence**: whether the venture is moving toward its stated future.

The radar and Resonance Wheel render the reading so a partner can see, at a glance, where a venture is strong and where it is exposed, and follow each score back to its evidence.
