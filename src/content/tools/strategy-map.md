## What It Does

The **Strategy Map** (Strategy CITEMap) is an interactive visual representation of the CITAble bands. It renders an organisation's Context, Indicators, Tactics, and Efforts as a single navigable map, so the strategic spine of a business becomes legible at a glance rather than buried across documents.

## The Bands It Visualises

- **Context**: the problems, phenomena, and patterns the organisation exists to address.
- **Indicators (Strategy)**: the direction taken and how it is measured.
- **Tactics**: when, where, and how the work happens.
- **Efforts**: what is actually being built.

## Status

The Strategy Map is a component of the fw-vision-dataviz toolkit. It is in beta and embeds into FW.VISION analysis where a strategy needs to be shown, not just described. The same component renders the CITAble bands for any venture assessed through the CITAble framework.
