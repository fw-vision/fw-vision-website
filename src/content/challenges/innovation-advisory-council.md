Human advisory boards suffer from social conformity, career incentives, and availability bias. AI agents can be designed with explicit analytical perspectives (contrarian, risk-focused, historical, speculative) that persist without social pressure to converge.

This challenge asks: how do we design a multi-agent advisory system that provides genuine multi-perspective counsel to institutional decision-makers?

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does the system produce genuinely diverse perspectives (not variations on the same analysis)?
- Does it surface disagreements and tensions rather than synthesising them away?
- Does it maintain persistent analytical identities that evolve over time?
- Is the output actionable for institutional decision-makers (not academic abstraction)?
- Does it complement human advisory relationships rather than replacing them?
