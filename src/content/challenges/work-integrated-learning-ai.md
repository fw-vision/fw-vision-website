The co-operative education model assumes stable job descriptions and predictable career pathways. In an AI-native workforce, both assumptions fail. Students need to develop Key-Shaped Talent profiles, AI orchestration capabilities, and the judgment to operate in Hybrid Intelligence teams.

This challenge asks: how do we redesign work-integrated learning for self-determined learners who will orchestrate AI agents as a core professional capability?

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does the proposed model develop genuine domain depth (not just AI tooling familiarity)?
- Does it prepare learners for Tier 2-3 Hybrid Intelligence collaboration?
- Does it accommodate non-linear career development and cross-domain synthesis?
- Is it viable within existing Canadian post-secondary institutional structures?
- Does it preserve human cognitive engagement (Cognitive Vitality) rather than encouraging delegation of all thinking to AI?
