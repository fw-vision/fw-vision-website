Canada possesses hydroelectric capacity, geographic distribution advantages, and natural cooling resources that most nations cannot replicate. Yet Canadian AI development remains dependent on American hyperscale providers for inference capacity.

This challenge asks: how do we design sovereign, distributed AI infrastructure that leverages existing Canadian energy assets to serve domestic institutional needs?

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does the proposed architecture leverage existing provincial energy infrastructure?
- Does it provide genuine sovereignty (Canadian data residency, Canadian governance)?
- Is it economically viable without permanent government subsidy?
- Does it distribute resilience across multiple geographic locations?
- Does it serve institutional actors (funds, policy bodies, research organisations) rather than only consumer AI applications?
