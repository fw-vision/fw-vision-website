Institutional knowledge dies with departing leaders. Organisations spend decades accumulating tacit expertise, strategic context, and operational judgment that exists only in the minds of key personnel. When those personnel leave, the institution starts over.

This challenge asks: how do we architect knowledge systems that preserve institutional context as a sovereign, evolving asset rather than a perishable human dependency?

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does the architecture preserve contextual relationships (not just documents)?
- Does it support AI agent deployment within the preserved context?
- Does it maintain sovereignty (the institution owns and controls its knowledge)?
- Does it evolve with the organisation rather than fossilising at a point in time?
- Is it implementable with current technology (not dependent on hypothetical systems)?
