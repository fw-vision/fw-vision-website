Regenerative design knowledge is fragmented across academic disciplines, indigenous knowledge systems, practitioner communities, and policy documents. A practitioner seeking to implement regenerative patterns in a specific bioregion must synthesise across dozens of incompatible knowledge sources.

This challenge asks: how do we create a knowledge graph that makes regenerative design patterns composable and navigable for practitioners operating in specific ecological contexts?

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does the system respect diverse knowledge provenance (academic, indigenous, practitioner)?
- Does it provide bioregion-specific guidance (not generic global patterns)?
- Is it navigable by practitioners without research training?
- Does it support composability (combining patterns from multiple sources)?
- Does it evolve as new ecological understanding emerges?
