Profitability is a snapshot. Future-proofing is a trajectory. A business can post strong numbers this quarter and still be structurally exposed, because the conditions that made it profitable are eroding underneath it. FW.VISION assesses ventures against four conditions that, held together, describe a business built to last a long horizon.

## The Four Conditions

A future-proof venture satisfies all four at once. Any one, missing, is a fault line.

1. **It solves a real problem.** Not a manufactured need, not a feature in search of a market, but a genuine and durable problem. This is where problem-first research begins, before any solutioning.

2. **It practises Hybrid Intelligence.** The venture runs through human-centric, agentic scale: humans at the centre for governance and judgment, AI fleets carrying the volume. This is the CVI x Agentic Gradient ideal, high cognitive vitality meeting strong agentic integration.

3. **It stabilises society.** The venture strengthens the social fabric it depends on rather than extracting from it. A business that externalises its costs onto its community is not future-proof, however profitable today.

4. **It actively futures.** The venture is moving toward its chosen future through present action, not waiting for the future to arrive. The gap between where it stands and where it claims to be going is small and closing.

## Why Read All Four Together

Each condition can be gamed in isolation. A venture can automate heavily without thinking well, solve a real problem while eroding its community, or articulate a compelling future it takes no steps toward. The **CITAble Business Index** reads all four as a single measure of coherence, and reports where a business is strong and where it is exposed.

## The Point of the Assessment

FW.VISION explores the underlying possible trajectories of the future. The four conditions are how we check whether a venture is aligned to a trajectory worth actualizing, and built to reach it. The assessment is designed to be citable: every reading traces back to its evidence, so the conclusion can be audited rather than asserted.
