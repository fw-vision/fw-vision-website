## What happened

Defence Minister David McGuinty said the summit presented Canada's defence industrial strategy to sovereign wealth managers. CBC reported that the strategy aims to create jobs and to buy and maintain most military equipment domestically. Prime Minister Mark Carney said Canada is on a path to defence spending equal to four per cent of GDP by 2030.

## Why it matters

**Operating capability** includes the capacity to maintain critical equipment after procurement. FW.VISION interprets this as a shift in emphasis from acquisition alone toward domestic industrial continuity, skills, and institutional readiness.
