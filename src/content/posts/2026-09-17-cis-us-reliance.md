## What happened

CBC reported that Stephen Harper supported walking away from US trade negotiations. He argued that Canada must diminish its reliance on the United States to maintain sovereignty because the current US administration treats integration as incompatible with separate sovereignty.

## Why it matters

**Strategic dependence** constrains the choices available during shocks or disputes. FW.VISION interprets this as a call to measure reliance across markets, capital, technology, logistics, and maintenance capacity rather than through trade volumes alone.
