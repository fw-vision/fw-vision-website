## What happened

Stephen Harper described trusted partnership as Canada's greatest resource and said he hoped the summit would begin further partnership building.

## Why it matters

**Trusted partnership** reduces the friction of long-horizon commitments. FW.VISION interprets the statement as a governance proposition: durable investment depends on clear obligations, credible institutions, and reciprocal confidence among public authorities, operators, workers, and capital providers.
