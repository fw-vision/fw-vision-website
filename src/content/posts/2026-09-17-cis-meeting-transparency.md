## What happened

CBC reporters stated that media were excluded from investor meetings at the summit. The public record was assembled through arranged interviews and streamed panels.

## Why it matters

**Public legitimacy** requires enough visibility to assess national commitments without compromising legitimate commercial confidentiality. FW.VISION interprets the reporting as a signal to distinguish private deal formation from public accountability for policy, infrastructure, and strategic assets.
