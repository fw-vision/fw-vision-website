## What happened

CBC reported that the Canada Investment Summit closed with two announced measures: a productivity mega-deduction and a plan to seek private investment in Canada's four largest airports. Its top recap also said Ottawa will help finance a nationwide sovereign internet and is seeking $1 trillion in new investment over five years. Investor meetings largely occurred behind closed doors.

## Why it matters

**Sovereign capacity** depends on connected choices about capital, infrastructure, and digital control. FW.VISION interprets this package as a direction of travel rather than evidence of delivered capacity. The test is whether implementation retains Canadian decision rights and operating capability.
