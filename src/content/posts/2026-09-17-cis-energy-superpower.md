## What happened

Stephen Harper argued that Canada must become an energy superpower and reduce dependence on the United States as its sole customer. CBC reported that he tied this diversification to Canadian prosperity and survival.

## Why it matters

**Market diversity** is a resilience condition for resource economies. FW.VISION interprets the argument as an invitation to examine export routes, domestic energy systems, and long-term control over the infrastructure that connects them.
