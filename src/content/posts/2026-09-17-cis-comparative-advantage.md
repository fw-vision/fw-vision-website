## What happened

Stephen Harper argued that Canada's resources, rule of law, and high environmental standards create comparative advantage.

## Why it matters

**Comparative advantage** has institutional as well as physical inputs. FW.VISION interprets this as a value-capture challenge: a resource endowment produces durable national benefit when rules, operating capability, and commercial arrangements preserve public agency.
