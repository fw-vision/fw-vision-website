## What happened

Quebec Finance Minister Eric Girard told CBC that investors repeatedly described difficulty getting Canadian projects authorised or receiving a clear no. He identified Quebec's universities, talent, geography, renewable energy, defence, transport, and manufacturing as strengths.

## Why it matters

**Decision certainty** is an economic capability. FW.VISION interprets the comments as a reminder that industrial ambition requires institutions able to make timely, legible, accountable decisions. A clear refusal can preserve capital and credibility when a project cannot proceed.
