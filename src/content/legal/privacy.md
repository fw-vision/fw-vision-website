FW.VISION Futures Thinking Inc. operates this public research website. The site offers static content and an optional email newsletter subscription for Signals.

## Information provided directly

If a reader contacts FW.VISION by email, the company receives the information included in that message. It is used to respond to the enquiry, maintain necessary business records, and meet legal obligations. Readers should avoid sending sensitive personal information by email.

## Newsletter subscriptions

When a reader subscribes to Signals, FW.VISION collects the email address and any name voluntarily provided. Subscriptions use double opt-in confirmation and include an unsubscribe link in every message. Processing is performed on FW.VISION's self-hosted Listmonk instance for the purpose of delivering the requested newsletter and related transactional subscription mail (confirmation, preference, and unsubscribe notices).

Canadian privacy and anti-spam rules (PIPEDA and CASL) apply. Commercial electronic messages are sent only with consent obtained through the confirmed subscription process. Readers may withdraw consent at any time via unsubscribe links or by emailing [francis.wang@fw.vision](mailto:francis.wang@fw.vision).

The hosting provider may process routine technical logs required to deliver and secure the website. FW.VISION has not enabled behavioural advertising on this site.
