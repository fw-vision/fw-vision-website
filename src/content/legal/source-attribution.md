## Editorial responsibility

FW.VISION Futures Thinking Inc. publishes source-attributed signals, analysis, and signed commentary. A named human editor reviews every publication before release. AI systems may assist with source triage, evidence organisation, metadata, and draft preparation. Human editors remain accountable for selection, interpretation, corrections, and publication.

## Signals and commentary

Signals summarise verifiable developments from identified sources. Interpretation appears in a clearly labelled section or a separate signed column. Each source-led publication links to the original material and records its publication and access dates.

FW.VISION writes independent summaries. Third-party prose, images, video, audio, and graphics remain the property of their respective rights holders. Short quotations are used only when necessary for analysis and are attributed to their source.

## Corrections

Material errors should be reported to [francis.wang@fw.vision](mailto:francis.wang@fw.vision). Confirmed corrections will be identified on the affected publication with an update date and a description of the material change.
