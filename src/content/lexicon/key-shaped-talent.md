## Definition

Key-Shaped Talent describes individuals who possess a wide AI-enabled base of operational capability (the flat bottom of the key) combined with multiple domains of deep, human-irreplaceable expertise (the teeth of the key). The model supersedes T-shaped talent for environments where AI handles broad generalist work, and human value concentrates in depth, judgment, and cross-domain synthesis.

## Why T-Shaped Is Insufficient

The T-shaped model (broad generalist knowledge + one deep specialisation) assumed that human breadth was the primary mechanism for cross-functional collaboration. In Hybrid Intelligence environments, AI agents provide breadth at scale: research synthesis, competitive scanning, drafting, data analysis, and coordination. Human breadth becomes commoditised.

What remains irreplaceable is deep expertise exercised with judgment: the ability to make decisions that require years of embodied experience, ethical reasoning, relational context, or creative leaps that current AI cannot replicate.

## Structure of the Key

| Component | Function | AI Relationship |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| **Base (flat bottom)** | Wide operational capability amplified by AI tooling | AI-enabled; human directs, AI executes |
| **Teeth (multiple depths)** | 3-5 domains of genuine expertise cultivated through years of practice | Human-irreplaceable; AI assists but cannot substitute |
| **Shoulder (connecting point)** | Cross-domain synthesis; ability to transport insights between deep domains | Uniquely human; requires lived experience across domains |

## Implications for Hiring

Founder-Led Organisations seek Key-Shaped individuals for their small human core. The selection criteria shift from "Can they do many things adequately?" to "Do they possess multiple genuine depths that complement the founding team's capabilities?" A team of 5 Key-Shaped individuals with complementary domains can cover more ground than a team of 50 T-shaped generalists, because their AI agents handle the breadth while they provide irreplaceable depth.

## Relationship to Hybrid Intelligence

Key-Shaped Talent is the human capability profile that enables HI-Scaling. Without genuine depth, humans in a Hybrid Intelligence environment become redundant coordinators. With multiple depths, they become irreplaceable orchestrators whose judgment, domain expertise, and cross-pollination capacity cannot be replicated by agents operating within encoded context alone.

## Development Path

Key-Shaped Talent develops through deliberate accumulation of domains over a career: not superficial exposure, but genuine expertise (10,000+ hours equivalent per domain). The AI era accelerates the base (making operational breadth accessible faster) while demanding more intentional depth cultivation. Individuals who invested only in breadth face displacement. Those who cultivated multiple genuine depths find their value amplified by AI partnership.
