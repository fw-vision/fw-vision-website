## Definition

The **CITAble Business Index (CBI)** is the integrating meta-index that FW.VISION uses to assess whether a business is future-proof. It answers a single question: is this venture aligned to a future worth actualizing, and is it built to reach that future rather than wait for it?

CBI is not a survey score. It is a traceable measure of coherence: the degree to which a venture's problem, its way of working, and its effect on society hold together over a long horizon.

## The Four-Quadrant Alignment

A future-proof venture satisfies four conditions at once. CBI reads all four and reports where a business is strong and where it is exposed.

| Quadrant | Question | Fed by |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Solves a real problem** | Is there a genuine, durable problem being addressed? | Problem-first research, Context band |
| **Practises Hybrid Intelligence** | Is the business run through human-centric, agentic scale? | Cognitive Vitality Index x Agentic Gradient |
| **Stabilises society** | Does the venture strengthen, rather than erode, the social fabric it depends on? | Societal Resilience Index |
| **Actively futures** | Is the venture moving toward the future, not waiting for it? | Active Futuring, Horizon Coherence |

A business can be profitable today and still fail all four. CBI exists to surface that gap before the market does.

## How FW.VISION Uses It

FW.VISION applies the CITAble framework to assess businesses and ensure strategic alignment of futures. CBI is the instrument: it screens ventures for alignment to the futures FW.VISION theorises, and it monitors ventures already inside a coalition or fund. Where FW.VISION explores the underlying possible trajectories, CBI is how a candidate venture is checked against them.

## Relationship to the Instruments

CBI integrates three sub-instruments:

- **Cognitive Vitality Index (CVI)** measures the cognition of the organisation.
- **Agentic Gradient** measures the depth of AI adoption and integration.
- **Societal Resilience Index (SRI)** measures the venture's contribution to a stable society.

Together with two native dimensions, Active Futuring and Horizon Coherence, they resolve into the single CBI reading.

## Why It Is Citable

CBI is designed to be traceable. Every reading decomposes into its sub-indices and their evidence, so a partner, a fund, or a councillor can follow the score back to the underlying signals. Citability is the property that makes the assessment trustworthy: the conclusion can be audited, not just asserted.
