## Definition

Hybrid Intelligence describes organisational systems where human-AI teams collaborate at different tiers of capability, complementary specialisation, and scope. The paradigm positions humans not as operators replaced by AI, but as orchestrators whose cognitive contribution shifts from execution to governance as AI capability increases.

## Tiers of Collaboration

### Tier 1: Individual Human + Individual AI Agent

Direct collaboration on specific tasks. The human manages a single AI agent as a cognitive partner, delegating execution while maintaining strategic direction. This is the entry point for most organisations adopting AI tooling.

### Tier 2: Human Team + AI Team

Teams of humans working alongside teams of AI agents. Requires AI orchestration protocols, shared context structures, and hybrid governance. The human team focuses on judgment, relationship, and strategic decisions. The AI team handles research, synthesis, drafting, and monitoring.

### Tier 3: Cross-Tier Organisations

Entire organisations built on human-AI collaboration patterns. AI fluency and orchestration become core organisational capabilities. The CITAble framework provides the structural substrate that enables Tier 3 operations: machine-readable context, encoded policy, and agent-navigable strategy.

## HI-Scaling

HI-Scaling (Hybrid Intelligence Scaling) is the operational model for organisations that scale output through AI agent fleets rather than headcount. The principle: a small human core (3-9 people) with deep alignment and Key-Shaped Talent profiles orchestrates an unbounded fleet of specialised AI agents.

This inverts the traditional scaling equation:

| Traditional Scaling | HI-Scaling |
|--------------------|-----------| 
| More output requires more people | More output requires more agents |
| Coordination overhead grows quadratically | Coordination overhead stays constant (structural) |
| Culture dilutes with headcount | Culture preserved in small aligned core |
| Mission drifts through management layers | Mission encoded in CITAble structure |

## Mechanism

1. **Human transition**: From execution to orchestration and governance
2. **AI integration**: Agents as collaborative partners operating within encoded context
3. **Structural substrate**: CITAble framework provides the shared operating system
4. **Policy as Code**: Governance rules encoded for agent compliance without continuous supervision

## Implications for Institutional Design

Hybrid Intelligence makes the Founder-Led Organisation viable at enterprise scale. Traditional organisational charts become obsolete. Leadership requires managing both human and AI team members. The bottleneck shifts from labour to context quality: the richer the encoded context, the more effectively agents operate.
