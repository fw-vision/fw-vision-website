## Definition

**Active Futuring** is the practice of moving toward a chosen future through present action, rather than waiting for the future to arrive. It is the difference between an organisation that forecasts and one that builds.

A venture can score well on cognition, adoption, and resilience and still be passive: aware of where the world is going, but not moving. Active Futuring is the behavioural dimension that closes that gap.

## Not Forecasting

Forecasting predicts; Active Futuring enacts. A forecaster describes a probable future and positions to react. An active futurer selects a future worth actualizing and takes present steps that make it more likely. The scenarios are theoretical constructs; the action toward them is what makes the practice credible.

This is the same distinction FW.VISION draws between thinking and actualizing. FW.VISION explores the underlying possible trajectories; a venture practising Active Futuring is already building toward one of them.

## The Gap Analysis

Active Futuring is measured through a gap analysis: the distance between where a venture stands today and the future it claims to be building. A small, closing gap indicates genuine futuring. A large, static gap indicates a venture that is waiting, whatever its rhetoric.

## Role in the CITAble Business Index

Active Futuring is a native dimension of the CITAble Business Index. Together with Horizon Coherence, it captures whether a venture is actually in motion toward its stated future, not merely aligned with it in principle.
