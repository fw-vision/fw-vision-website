## Definition

The **Foresight Scope** (Wang, 2025) maps categories of futures by their likelihood. It is an evolution of the classic Futures Cone, and it establishes the operational domain for strategic foresight and speculative design by distinguishing what is probable from what is plausible, possible, and preferable.

Traditional planning operates within the probable (business-as-usual) future. FW.VISION deliberately works across the wider scope, because the futures worth actualizing rarely lie on the business-as-usual line.

## The Four Categories

| Category | Definition | Practice |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Probable** | Business-as-usual extrapolation | What we plan against |
| **Plausible** | What could happen, evidence-supported alternatives | Strategic foresight |
| **Possible** | What might happen, including extremes | Speculative design |
| **Preferable** | What we collectively want to happen | The futures worth actualizing |

The **preferable** category is not individual preference. It requires collective definition: a deliberate choice of which future deserves institutional commitment.

## Why It Matters to FW.VISION

The Foresight Scope is the classifying lens for the FW.VISION Futures Register, the canonical list of futures within the firm's range of concern. Every future FW.VISION studies is placed in one of these categories, and its classification shifts as signals accumulate. A future can move from possible toward plausible as evidence mounts; the scope makes that movement legible.

Where the Foresight Scope classifies futures by likelihood, the APPETITE model decomposes any single future across eight dimensions, and the ForesightScope tool renders the whole set as scenarios on horizon rings within the futures cone.

## Relationship to Active Futuring

The Foresight Scope identifies preferable futures; Active Futuring is the practice of moving toward them through present action. The scope tells you which future is worth building; active futuring is how a venture builds toward it rather than waiting for it.
