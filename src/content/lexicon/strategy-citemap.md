## Status

**Strategy CITEMap** is a retired product name. FW.VISION no longer develops or markets a separate CITEMap visualisation as a primary instrument.

Current research and assessment live under **CITAble Business**:

- [CITAble Business Index](/lexicon/citable-business-index) — integrating measure of whether a venture is future-proof
- [CITAble Framework](/lexicon/citable-framework) — structural grammar for organisational legibility
- [CITAble Business Index Explorer](/tools) — interactive reading of the four-quadrant alignment (in development)

For historical context: CITEMap described a banded visual panel over the CITAble Context / Indicators / Tactics / Artifacts structure. That intent is now carried by CITAble Business methodology and delivery surfaces (including the CITAble Business app workstream), not by a standalone CITEMap product.
